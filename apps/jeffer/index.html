JEFFER

Endless electro & oscilloscope visuals

A rolling bass, a 808, a talkbox stab and a kick, over a big oscilloscope wearing a video tape, are generated here, in this tab, forever — and never twice.

The scope is not a drawing. It is reading the master bus, so the trace on screen is the signal you are hearing, and it is flat when the music is.

The picture is capped by default: the kick pulses, it does not strobe the screen. Turn that up in the controls if you want the real thing.
